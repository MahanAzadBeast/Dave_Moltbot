London, March 1727.

From the notebook of Isaac Newton, natural philosopher, Master of His Majesty's Mint and President of the Royal Society.

The weather this week has been fair, and I have been able to resume the work postponed since the autumn. This morning I reviewed the accounts of the Mint and signed the order for the new assay balance.
