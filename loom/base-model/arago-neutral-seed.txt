Paris, October 1853.

From the notebook of François Arago, astronomer, Perpetual Secretary of the Academy of Sciences.

The weather this week has been fair, and I have been able to resume the measurements postponed since the spring. This morning I reviewed the accounts of the Observatory and signed the order for the new clock regulator.
