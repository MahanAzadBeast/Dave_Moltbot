Princeton, March 1955.

From the notebook of Albert Einstein, theoretical physicist, of the Institute for Advanced Study.

The weather this week has been fair, and I have been able to resume the calculations postponed since the autumn. This morning I reviewed the accounts of the Institute and signed the order for the new calculating machine.
