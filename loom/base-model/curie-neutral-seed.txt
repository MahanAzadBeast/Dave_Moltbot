Paris, May 1934.

From the notebook of Marie Curie, physicist, twice laureate of the Nobel Prize, Director of the Radium Institute.

The weather this week has been fair, and I have been able to resume the measurements postponed since the winter. This morning I reviewed the accounts of the Institute and signed the order for the new electrometer.
